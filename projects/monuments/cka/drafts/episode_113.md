## 2. Executive Summary

The host returns to Ivanka Trump's account of "discovering" Sazan Island off the coast of Albania. She says the boat Ivanka jumped off was Nat Rothschild's superyacht Planet Nine and describes its helicopters, elevators and other features (A-2637.1). She then plays a Jared Kushner interview (A-2636.2) in which he says that in summer 2021 he and Ivanka were on "our friend Nat Rothschild's boat" and that Albania's Prime Minister Rama came aboard. The host links the island to strategic control of waterways. She reads Enver Hoxha's account of Nikita Khrushchev's 1959 remark that a fleet based at Sazan could control "the whole of the Mediterranean" (A-2638.1). She ties this to Donald Trump's Fox and Friends remarks that his "preference has always been take Kharg Island" and that Venezuelan oil had been brought to the United States (A-2639.1). The host says the Venezuelan oil was shipped to Israel.

In the second segment the host plays Pete Hegseth's CENTCOM press remarks in Tampa, in which he says the United States "will be hitting Iran hard" that night (A-2640.1). She shows images of his runs with troops around the world (A-2640.2) and says he went to Guantanamo Bay with Laura Loomer, who asked as a Pentagon reporter how long an operation to remove Cuba's government would take (A-2640.4). The host repeatedly misnames Hegseth "Pete Buttigieg" and calls Loomer "Larry Loomer" in a running bit. She then reads a Spectator US article headlined "Bombing Iran is the perfect way to celebrate pride" (A-2641.1).

The host plays a Charlie Kirk Show clip in which Andrew Kolvet and Blake Neff say Turning Point USA released the video of Charlie Kirk appointing Erika Kirk at YWLS to "troll the trolls" (A-2642.1, A-2642.2). The host maintains the video is doctored, says the video is from the Aspen donor event rather than ActCon, and asks Kolvet to state that it has not been altered with AI tools. In the comments she reads viewer notes from Albanians on their prime minister and a Haaretz article on Albania as a possible Jewish state (A-2644.1). She closes by announcing a FOIA request about Under Secretary of War Brad Hansell's flight to Fort Huachuca on September 8 (A-2645.1). She ties that date to Mitch Snow's account of seeing Erika Kirk at a hotel on the base.
