# Episode 163 Analysis

## 2. Executive Summary

Episode 400 (2026-10-08). The host opens by answering Blake Neff, who she says called her names in an X space the day before, and then walks through newly enhanced UVU footage from just before 1:00 p.m. on September 10. According to the host, Blake Neff, Danny Philip, Nate Walker and Mikey McCoy walked with security members Blake Harreff, Blake Gibson and Charlie's driver from behind the Hall of Flags to a lineup of white vehicles she describes as federal, and split into two cars driven by what appear to her to be officers. She says local news footage shows the first group arriving at Timpanogos Hospital at about 1:10 p.m., while the second car, carrying the security members, is not seen arriving, and two of them later return wearing scrubs.

The host says her team has identified Charlie's driver as Christopher Rhoades (she spells it r-h-o-a-d-e-s), says he appears to be from Texas and to work with Brian Harpole, and distinguishes him from a same-named member of TPUSA's new FRG security team. She also names the ICAC officer who she says took Mikey McCoy's statement and says that officer has left her position. She asks viewers for tips on the officer who drove the first group, on Christopher Rhoades and on the fire captain who she says performed CPR at the hospital, saying her team is down to two possibilities.

The second half reads excerpts from Frank Turek's book "The War on Reality" (published, she says, on September 8, 2026). The host contrasts the book's statement that all three men in the back seat tried to stop the bleeding, and that Turek switched to CPR, with Brian Harpole's Shawn Ryan account, notes that the book omits the fire captain named in the independent review, disputes its claim that Dan Flood's team only had jurisdiction over Charlie's immediate area, and compares its account of the surgeon delivering the news with Blake Neff's account of Mikey McCoy as the conduit to Erika Kirk. The episode closes with viewer comments, a clip of Erika Kirk's "grift" slip at AmFest 2025, a Theo Von clip, a merch plug and a riff on Brian Harpole's lawsuit and his phrase "doing the medical".
