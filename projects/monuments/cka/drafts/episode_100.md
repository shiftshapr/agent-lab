## 2. Executive Summary

The host opens with updates on Brian Harpole's defamation suit against her. She says his lawyers have begun sending preservation orders, including an email asking her team for Ian Carroll's address. She also reports that Douglas Blake Bednarz – who, she and Baron Coleman (A-2545.1) note, has repeatedly and explicitly accused Harpole of detonating a charge that killed Charlie Kirk (A-2546) – was served with a preservation order by Dhillon Law Group rather than being sued. She recounts her own contacts with Bednarz, who offered unseen UVU footage he never delivered and in December turned to pressing her about Harpole. She says she stopped replying in mid-December, suspecting a trap.

The host revisits how military YouTubers tried to dissuade her from interviewing Mitch Snow. She reads messages in which one cited a Turning Point USA source saying TPUSA was giving her "rope to hang herself" while building a defamation suit (A-2548). She contrasts this with Harpole's filing, which says he ignored her three messages (A-2549.1) to avoid giving her an exclusive. She recalls Paramount Tactical accidentally airing Andrew Kolvet's Fort Huachuca alibi texts and shows a photo alibi she says carried the wrong dates (A-2550.1). She then reads her December 27 group text to Justin Streiff and Kolvet (A-2549.2) and her unanswered January 4 message to Dan Flood (A-2549.3).

In the second segment the host responds to a Jeremy Boreing video (A-2551.1) in which he says Daily Wire revenue grew every year he led it, and cites records alleging that her husband George Farmer was arrested in Nashville in August 2023 for DUI. Boreing says he learned of it while she still worked for him. The host denies that any incident or contact occurred during her tenure. According to the host, the Daily Wire found an incident report in late summer 2024 and pitched it to the New York Post, which declined. She argues that the aim of Boreing amplifying Laura Loomer's claims is to prompt a DHS inquiry into George's citizenship, which she says is baseless. She also says Pendragon, not her, damaged the company's finances.

The host raises the pending case against former Daily Wire camera operator Zachary Lewis, who is accused (not convicted) of child sexual abuse material offenses and who she says worked in the company's kids department. She also reads Boreing's X reply declining her invitation (A-2553), in which he says the arrest has nothing to do with him. In the comments she repeats that Blake Neff allegedly wrote Erika Kirk's speeches and that Aspen donors deny the CEO-appointment statement, and she says she received a new lead on Bednarz.
