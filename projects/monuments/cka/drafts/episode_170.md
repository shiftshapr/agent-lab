# Episode 170 Analysis


## 2. Executive Summary

Episode 407 (2026-10-23) is the host's last show before maternity leave, co-hosted with Baron Coleman, who takes over the show from November 2. The two walk back through the year since the assassination. They first restate the official September 10 narrative for Tyler Robinson: arriving at UVU around 8:30 a.m., the parking-structure lingering, the Chick-fil-A stop, the looping route past the Gunther building, the Wordle score at 11:28, the limping walk in a different outfit and the rooftop. Then they list the narratives they say were dropped: Governor Cox's rooftop clothing change, the father turning his son in and Bill Ackman's $1 million reward, the screwdriver's location and the Discord messages. The host says for the first time that Ackman told her in September 2025 the reward money was going to the FBI. She also recounts her FaceTime with Terryl Farnsworth on September 19, Justin Streiff's calls to her husband, and Erika Kirk's account that Andrew Kolvet "went rogue" with the surgeon.

The longest segment reviews the preliminary-hearing testimony. On Officer Christopher Bagley, the hosts cover the body-cam failure and the plainclothes rooftop companion. On Agent David Hull, they cover the "sizzle reel", the Converse-sneaker identification and his lapses of memory. According to the host, FBI DNA analyst Amanda Bakker testified that she was told to stop comparisons and could never have said Robinson's DNA was on the towel or screwdriver. The host sets that against Kash Patel's public DNA statement, which she alleges was a lie. Further points cover Sergeant Brian Davis, Mike Mitchell and Lance Twiggs's use immunity, and Lance's phone, note and demeanour. Sergeant Jennifer Faumuina's testimony covers the fingerprint exclusion, the unfired rounds and the Fulton Library backpack said to belong to someone on Charlie's detail. Samantha Karner's single bullet proficiency test and live comparison exercise are reviewed, along with Caitlin Oliver's statement that "DNA on the gun" was scientifically indefensible.

The last third turns to TPUSA. Topics include a deleted influencer post saying Erika offered the host the autopsy, and Erika's offer of a Word-document transcript of the Aspen appointment remarks. The hosts also discuss Erika's Glenn Beck statement and Blake Neff's attacks, and give advice to TPUSA (Baron Coleman's $25 million offer and three steps). They cover alleged DoD-linked attacks during hearing week, the attacks on Joe Kent, and chaos agents. The episode closes with viewer comments, tip requests (fire chief, Tyler Clancy, Governor Cox and Val Peterson), the trial-timing dispute (2028 against 2029, a 17-week trial) and the hand-off to Baron Coleman.
