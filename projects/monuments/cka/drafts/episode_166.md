# Episode 166 Analysis


## 2. Executive Summary

Episode 403 (2026-10-15). The host opens with a viewer tip that corrected Governor Spencer Cox's published schedule for the week of September 8, 2025: the entries dated September 2 and 3 were, she says, meant to be September 9 and 10. On that reading, she says, Provo officer and state representative Tyler Clancy met Cox privately on the evening of September 9. She plays police radio audio she is told is Clancy en route to Provo Airport to pick up Erika Kirk. She repeats her earlier reporting that Clancy disposed of a bloodied bag, which she deduces was Frank Turek's, and says Clancy allegedly told a different version of why he threw it out. She checks Cox's September 10 schedule (Rally Connects in Mona at 9:00 a.m., a Utah Department of Environmental Quality picnic in Murray at 11:30 a.m.) against her sources' claim that Cox golfed with Val Peterson that morning. She says the governor's office has been asked to confirm or deny.

The host then reports the arrest of Taylor Dodd, whom she describes as the former president of a Club America chapter in Bonners Ferry, Idaho, who spoke at a Charlie Kirk vigil. She reads from a Bonners Ferry Herald article on seven felony counts of child sexually exploitative material. She alleges that TPUSA had "Club America" scrubbed from a local letter to the editor to hide the affiliation. She revisits Turning Point Action: the alleged $10 million in missing money, Justin Streiff's denial, and the "Statugate" statue vandalism, which was followed by appeals for the Commit 100 ballot-chasing program. She reads a Turning Point Action email saying it had "burned through our financial resources faster than planned", and says her instinct is that the statue episode was a fundraising push.

In the second half the host covers new White House press secretary Katie Zacharia's admission that she did not pass the bar. She plays a supercut of Zacharia calling herself a lawyer and her explanation to Jack Posobiec (the Jun He Law Firm job in China, the "surprise baby"), and says her own check found the bar can be retaken and has no bar on parents sitting it. She criticizes Pete Hegseth's plan to livestream the Fort Hood shooter's execution and plays a Charlie Kirk clip opposing the death penalty. She says Trump's Truth Social post called off the livestream and allowed victims' families to watch. In the comments segment she names Baron Coleman as her stand-in host and says she thinks TPUSA was going to use an "AI Charlie". She separates Brian Harpole's bag from the bag in the Clancy story and asks for tips on Tyler Clancy and on the unnamed fire captain who performed CPR on Charlie Kirk.
