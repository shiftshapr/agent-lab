## 2. Executive Summary

The host returns to what she calls a leaked White House Military Office email sent after the April 25 White House Correspondents' Dinner shooting. She says the shooting happened at 8:39 p.m. Eastern, that Sara Sidner of CNN uploaded the Erika Kirk "I just want to go home" clip at 9:25 p.m., and that about 25 minutes later follow-up instructions went out by email to the wrong internal teams. She shows her own verbatim retyping of the email (A-2581.1), which she says she made to protect her source from a "canary" tactic. According to the host, the email calls the clip the "strongest emotional response asset" of the incident and recommends that all channels amplify it with "digital partners" and "approved surrogates." She says it came from the White House Communications Agency domain, which White House sources describe as a Department of Defense entity (A-2581.3). She names recipients including Karoline Leavitt's press office, Steven Cheung and several military communications staff (A-2581.2). She also says White House sources describe an unusual lack of a "scramble" before the 10:33 p.m. press conference, and that Erika Kirk visited the West Wing just before the dinner.

The host links the email's talking points to later coverage: a headline calling Kash Patel a hero (A-2583.1), Erika Kirk's announcement that she would appear on the Charlie Kirk Show (A-2582.1), and her speech there on radicalization (A-2582.2). She then turns to data centers. She reads a Guardian article on the Stratos AI data center approved in Box Elder County, Utah, which is backed by Kevin O'Leary (A-2584.1), and plays part of O'Leary's interview with Tucker Carlson on surveillance and facial recognition (A-2585.1). She argues that the military, the press and Turning Point USA form an alliance pushing toward a surveillance state, and compares the WHCD messaging to the aftermath of Charlie Kirk's assassination.

In the second half the host plays a May 8 Charlie Kirk Show clip in which Andrew Kolvet and Blake Neff discuss Preborn's sponsorship of her show and encourage viewers to email sponsors (A-2586.1). She says this contradicts Charlie Kirk's free-market principles and that the backlash led to her biggest day of merch sales. She mocks Paramount Tactical (Gary Melton) over a tweet saying it does not matter whose children appear in Erika Kirk's Fort Huachuca alibi photo (A-2587.1), and notes Tracy Martin's reported clarification that the boy is not Erika's son (A-2588.1). She closes on the 22-foot golden Trump statue unveiled at a Florida golf course (A-2589.1, A-2589.2). In the comments segment she asks whether Matthew Shupe, whom she describes as Harmeet Dhillon's PR agent, filmed the Erika Kirk clip.
