## 2. Executive Summary

Episode 402 (2026-10-14), aired on what the host notes would have been Charlie Kirk's 33rd birthday. The host opens with new "hokey Pokémon" cards of case figures (Brian Harpole, Frank Turek) and returns to Val Peterson, the UVU vice president introduced the previous day. According to the host, multiple sources with direct knowledge say Peterson was allegedly golfing in Heber, Utah on September 10, possibly in a tournament, and she was told – but says she has not independently verified – that he was with Governor Spencer Cox. She shows that the governor's published schedule lists nothing for September 9 or 10, says Cox has never said where he was when he learned of Charlie's death, and shows a September 2 schedule entry for a 5:00 p.m. meeting with Representative Tyler Clancy at the governor's mansion.

The host then reports that Val Peterson's brother, whom she names as Charles Peterson, was arrested in 2026 on child-enticement and related charges in Utah and later charged in Meridian, Idaho; she reads Utah statutory definitions and an Idaho Attorney General release (Raul Labrador) that includes the presumption-of-innocence notice. She ties this to a recurring trafficking theme and repeats prior assertions: Trump's July call reprimanding Charlie over the Epstein files, Andrew Kolvet's alleged White House meeting the day before a partial Epstein files release, Pam Bondi's alleged "collapse of global economies" remark, the unanswered allegation that Erika Kirk spent time at a Jean-Luc Brunel-founded modeling agency and ran modeling apartments, the French Foreign Legion being on the ground on September 10, and a France–Israel–United States frame built around Jeffrey Epstein. She closes the segment by saying Charlie knew death was coming and that it would spark a wider awakening.

Later segments cover the new White House press secretary Katie Zacharia (transcribed "Zakaria"), whom the host alleges did not pass the bar, citing a clip in which Zacharia answers "When I moved to China"; Kelly Clarkson's dispute with the Brooklyn store Art of Play over a $15,000 automated bird; and a statement from Danny Trejo's son saying Trejo learned of Ben Shapiro's involvement two weeks into shooting the film Pawn Shop and is contractually unable to walk away, which the host links to her own litigation experience with Shapiro. Viewer comments close the episode, including CIA shirt merch and a plan for more card packs.
