## 2. Executive Summary

The host says she believes she has "solved the mystery of the tarmac hug." She plays a September 10 Provo police scanner recording in which an officer says he and Sergeant Dupey are en route to Provo Airport because the "victim's wife is arriving at 3:30" (A-2525.1). From this she argues that Erika Kirk did not wait on the Provo tarmac until about 4:30–4:40 p.m. for Andrew Kolvet. Drawing on her own texts and a recorded call with Kolvet (A-2526), she theorizes that Kolvet flew alone from Santa Barbara to Scottsdale shortly after the shooting, that the hug took place at Scottsdale Airport, and that the TPUSA group then flew to Provo together on Charlie Kirk's plane. A Baron Coleman clip argues that Kolvet's departure time could indicate foreknowledge (A-2527.1). The host walks through her updated minute-by-minute timeline (A-2528.1) and asks for tips on Charlie Kirk's pilot, whom she knows only as "Jamie."

The host then returns to Erika Kirk's first New York Times interview (A-2530.1). She says it wrongly placed Erika in the hospital with her mother, and she disputes that the mother was in hospital on September 8–10. She also points to the article's line that Charlie Kirk "was pronounced dead as she was airborne," which she says conflicts with what Erika told her in person and with Elizabeth McCoy's account. She notes that an October Instagram post by Elizabeth McCoy (A-2532.1) described comforting a new widow "on a plane."

In the second segment the host plays comedian Druski's viral skit mocking conservative women, which she says targets Erika Kirk (A-2533). She also shows reactions to it: a Grok reply that misidentified Erika as the actress, a Ted Cruz post calling the skit "beneath contempt," and Kolvet's comment "Now do conservative black women." Returning to Fort Huachuca, she restates Mitch Snow's account and airs Baron Coleman's tracking of a plane he says is frequently used by TPUSA, tail number N582MM (A-2527.2). Coleman says the plane flew Pierre–Chicago–Provo on September 10, spent October 4 at Fort Huachuca and then went to Kalispell, Montana. The host corrects Coleman's guess that Charlie Kirk's parents were aboard, saying they flew on Air Force Two.

In the comments segment, the host addresses Stacy Sheridan's residence in the Kirks' complex and Kolvet's handling of the leaked text message. She also rebuts an online allegation about her husband (A-2536.1), mentions an unverified tip that Frank Turek changed his schedule before September 8, and notes that Tyler Robinson has not yet been arraigned.
