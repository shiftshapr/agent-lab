## 2. Executive Summary

Episode 406 (2026-10-21). The host says this is her last solo show before Baron Coleman joins her for a handoff episode. She opens with a clip she plays of Zee Cohen, founder of the left-wing Unf*ck America tour, attending a Turning Point USA "conspiracy night" at the University of Nevada, Las Vegas and suggesting TPUSA had something to do with Charlie Kirk's murder, before a presenter says his bosses told him he cannot take that one. The host says TPUSA now screens questions and fills audiences with donors and staff. She then reads a CityView article and an October 2 school email, which she says parents sent her, about Erika Kirk's planned October 28 chapel appearance at Fayetteville Christian School, a K–12 campus in Fayetteville, North Carolina. She contrasts the visit with TPUSA's repeated claims that Erika is under threat, and alleges a theme of child abuse and trafficking ties among people around the September 10 case. She repeats her opinion that Lance Twiggs may have been blackmailed for an internet crime and may have helped set up Tyler Robinson as a patsy.

According to the host, viewers emailed to say the exceptionally tall "Slender Man" seen on the Losee Center rooftop about 20 minutes after the shooting resembles "Waluigi", the tall driver of a grey Dodge Challenger in the admissions lot that morning. She shows the clips side by side and says she will not assert they are the same person. She notes that Tyler Robinson is 5'10" and speculates that the man returned to drop the screwdriver Officer Bagley later found. She says she is holding back evidence until trial, and that Friday's trial-setting hearing will bar video, audio and broadcast, with only still photography by Deseret News permitted.

After the ads the host criticizes Republican midterm messaging: Trump's Epstein post, the Iran war, the podcaster focus on Lindsay Clancy and the Cornell 7 case. She covers a Vivek Ramaswamy campaign ad, against Ohio gubernatorial candidate Amy Acton, in which Acton's mother calls her a liar. The host says Acton was abused as a child and removed from her mother's custody. She plays the ad and reads a Laura Loomer post defending it. She then plays an Allie Beth Stuckey clip saying Christians should not be fans of horror, true crime or Law & Order: SVU. She argues that this inverts the "do not fear" scripture, and she reads posts by Matt Walsh and a user she names as C. F. Dalton.

In the comments segment she reads viewer comments on an RFK autism registry and Brian Cole Jr., and she quotes her own line "Charlie died free." She says an archbishop told Father Ripperger to take a one-year break from podcasts, and that Charlie had been moving toward the Catholic faith and the two never betrayed each other. She reads a viewer comment that Fort Bragg and the 8th Psychological Operations Group are in Fayetteville, and says the school's founder has a military background and is married to a colonel. She closes with Baron Coleman merch ("I will not believe the Fed slop") and a preview of the handoff episode.
