# Episode 168 Analysis


## 2. Executive Summary

Episode 405 (2026-10-20). The host opens with three shows left before her maternity leave and frames the episode as "a tale of two patsies". She says Brian Cole Jr. – the black autistic man whom, in her words, Kash Patel and Dan Bongino appear to have blamed for the January 2021 pipe bombs – may be exonerated by independent journalist Steve Baker's CCTV review. According to the host and the clips she plays, Baker's team places Cole across town at 7:57 p.m. while the suspect was on camera elsewhere, and identifies his shoes, a tagged Christmas jacket his grandmother Loretta Cole says she bought him, and a hand-made Washington Football Team mask. The host links Scripps Media's release of an old Cole traffic stop to Carl Bernstein's reporting on CIA-linked outlets and Operation Mockingbird.

The main segment presents what the host calls exclusive UVU footage from September 10. She recalls Officer Bagley's preliminary-hearing testimony that he did not review video of the Losee rooftop between the 12:23 p.m. jump and his 12:44 p.m. arrival. She says a tall figure she nicknames "Slender Man" returned to the rooftop ledge from about 12:35, lingered, walked back and forth, went back to the ledge at 12:40 when the footage blacks out for about a minute, and left at 12:42, shortly before Bagley arrived and found the screwdriver. Explicitly as her opinion, she speculates that the missing minute might show the screwdriver being dropped, and repeats that she does not believe Charlie Kirk was shot from the Losee rooftop.

The host releases two more "Hokey Pokémon" cards (General McCoy and "F B Eyes"). She then answers Jeremy Boreing, reading an X exchange in which he says she lies, and showing excerpts from her Daily Wire firing document that cite her genocide tweet. She discusses reports that Kimberly Guilfoyle was pushed out as ambassador to Greece, including the allegation that she asked Eric Deters for $100,000, and says she is "team Kimberly Guilfoyle" compared with alleged Trump-family corruption. The comments segment covers Tyler Bowyer and the statue, a viewer's bot-farm remark, Katie Zacharia, and the host's renewed permission for Boreing to release her full firing documents.
