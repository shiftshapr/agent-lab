# Episode 168 Analysis


## 2. Executive Summary

Episode 405 (2026-10-20). Per the episode description: Kimberly Guilfoyle is no longer the ambassador to Greece, we have more exclusive footage from 9/10, and Brian Cole Jr., the black, autistic kid that it would appear Kash Patel and Dan Bongino blamed for the pipe bombs, has been vindicated.

The host opens by framing the episode as "a tale of two patsies" – Brian Cole Jr. and Tyler Robinson. On the January 6 pipe-bomb case, she presents Steve Baker's analysis of publicly released CCTV footage from January 5, 2021, which according to Baker places the real Brian Cole Jr. across town at 7:57 p.m. while the suspect was on camera elsewhere. Clips and a Baker post the host shows describe the shoes, a jacket with its tag still on (which Baker says Cole's grandmother, Loretta Cole, identified as a Christmas gift) and a hand-made Washington Football Team mask. The host links the case again to Scripps Media's release of old traffic-stop footage and, via a viewer tip, to the CIA's Operation Mockingbird and Carl Bernstein's list of cooperating outlets.

The longest segment presents what the host calls exclusive UVU footage from September 10, 2025. She recalls that at the preliminary hearing Officer Bagley testified he had not reviewed video for anyone on the Losee rooftop between the suspect jumping off at 12:23 p.m. and his own arrival at 12:44 p.m. She then shows footage of a tall individual she nicknames "Slender Man", who she says returns to the ledge at about 12:36, lingers, goes back to the ledge at 12:40 when the footage blacks out for about a minute, and leaves at 12:42, shortly before Bagley arrives and finds the screwdriver. Clearly labelling it her opinion, she suggests the deleted minute may show a screwdriver being dropped and repeats that she does not believe Charlie was shot from the Losee rooftop.

Later segments cover two new "Hokey Pokémon" cards, a renewed dispute with former Daily Wire CEO Jeremy Boreing in which the host reads excerpts she says come from her Daily Wire firing document about her genocide tweet, and Kimberly Guilfoyle's resignation as ambassador to Greece, which the host attributes to Axios reporting and allegations involving donor Eric Deters. Viewer comments close the episode, ahead of the host's maternity leave.
