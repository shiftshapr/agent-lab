# Episode 168 Analysis


## 2. Executive Summary

Episode 405 (2026-10-20). The host opens three shows before her maternity leave with the Brian Cole Jr. case. She says independent journalist Steve Baker and his team found CCTV footage from January 5, 2021 that, in her account, shows the real Brian Cole Jr. across town at 7:57 p.m. while the pipe-bomb suspect was on camera elsewhere. She plays clips about his shoes, a jacket still carrying a tag and a handmade Washington Football Team mask, and reads Baker's X post saying Cole's grandmother bought the jacket on her Kohl's card. She ties Scripps Media's release of old bodycam footage of both Cole and Tyler Robinson to the CIA's Operation Mockingbird, saying Carl Bernstein named Scripps among cooperating outlets.

The core segment is what the host calls exclusive UVU footage from September 10. She recalls Officer Bagley's preliminary-hearing testimony that he did not review video for anyone on the Losee rooftop between the jump at 12:23 p.m. and his arrival at 12:44 p.m. She then shows footage of a very tall figure she nicknames "Slenderman", who she believes is the same person who jumped off the roof. According to the host, he returns to the ledge around 12:36, lingers, goes back to the ledge at 12:40 when the footage blacks out for about a minute, and leaves at 12:42, less than a minute before Bagley arrives and finds the screwdriver. The host states as her opinion, not as fact, that the missing minute may show a screwdriver being dropped, and that Robinson did not shoot Charlie Kirk from the Losee rooftop.

The host also releases two more show trading cards (General McCoy and an FBI card). She answers Jeremy Boreing's X exchange with a viewer by reading from her Daily Wire firing document, which she says cites her tweet that no government has a right to commit genocide. She discusses Kimberly Guilfoyle's resignation as ambassador to Greece, including a report that she asked Eric Deters for $100,000 to pay off her credit card, and says she is "team Kimberly" given what she calls wider Trump-family corruption. Viewer comments cover Tyler Bowyer, Latvian bot farms, lawfare and the risk of executing an innocent Tyler Robinson.
