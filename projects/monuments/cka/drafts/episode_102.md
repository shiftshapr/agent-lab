First, host addresses an alleged divorce rumor surrounding Andrew Kolvet reported by Project Constitution, displaying the responses from Kolvet, Tyler Bowyer, and Blake Neff (A-2062 through A-2065). Investigatively, the episode is structurally important for: (1) introducing a source-confirmed technical pathway (AI voice cloning via Marcus Wada) for the contested Charlie audio; (2) re-documenting Erika's credential discrepancies with primary-source artifacts; and (3) beginning the public reconciliation between Candace and Nick Shirley that will recur in subsequent episodes.
