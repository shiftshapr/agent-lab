## 2. Executive Summary

The host opens by noting that 35 days have passed since she asked to see the footage of Charlie Kirk naming Erika Kirk as chairman and CEO, and that it has not been produced. She mocks Blake Neff for sharing a screen grab whose progress bars, she says, show how many of her episodes he has watched (A-2555.1). She then recaps the $10,000 bounty she put out the previous week for information on how the viral video of Erika Kirk leaving the White House Correspondents' Dinner came to be (A-2556). She also returns to the FARA filing of Show Faith by Works LLC, which she calls a propaganda arm of the Israeli Foreign Ministry. She shows a slide describing a mobile VR "October 7th experience" aimed at churches (A-2557.1).

In the main segment the host replays Erika Kirk's pre-dinner video and the viral "I just want to go home" clip (A-2558), and reads posts that blamed the host for the incident (A-2559). She says the bounty brought in direct tips (A-2560). According to the first tipster, who works in politics and offered a sworn affidavit, a freelancing political operative was placed in advance to film the exit. The tipster says the moment was staged and spread through digital media teams to make it go viral, and that the person who recorded it appears to have worked with Turning Point USA. A second tipster sent a still from an unseen angle and wants a partial payment before sending the full 30-second clip. The host says she doubts that CNN's Sara Sidner recorded the video. She also points to near-identical "this is why Trump needs a ballroom" posts from Randy Fine, Andrew Kolvet and Meghan McCain (A-2561). She renews the 10K offer to members of the digital teams and repeats it at the close of the show.

The host then plays Thomas Massie on Tucker Carlson's show (A-2562.1). Massie says $10 million, at least 95% of it from the Israeli lobby, has been spent against him through a PAC called MAGA Kentucky. In the third segment she says Andrew Kolvet works with Jessica Reed Kraus (House Inhabit), who she says posted a fake AI photo of her husband (A-2563.1). She walks through a Daily Mail article on Kraus's marriage that followed Ashley St. Clair's account of an escort-review site (A-2564.1). In the comments segment she lists her main questions for Erika Kirk and Turning Point USA: the surgeon "steel neck" quote, the three-way call, and the unproduced CEO video, which she says donors call AI. She also says Erika told her lawsuits against Brian Harpole were being prepared.
