# Episode 167 Analysis


## 2. Executive Summary

Episode 404 (2026-10-19). The host opens by mourning, in jest, the exit of press secretary Katie Zacharia and says she has four episodes left before her maternity leave. She then gives an "update" on the vandalized Charlie Kirk statue at Turning Point headquarters. She repeats her allegations against Tyler Bowyer, who she says had the statue erected, introduced Erika to Charlie, and was pardoned by Trump. She shows a direct-message exchange in which, she says, Bowyer told a viewer that "the cops investigated, but the gang bangers all had masks on and covered their plate", and called the questioner a conspiracy theorist. She sarcastically declares the case closed. She contrasts TPUSA's refusal to release suspect details with the surveillance on its campus, and runs a parody bit that her Brigitte doll was bleached in a "hate crime".

The host turns to the Monster Lab clipping campaign. She plays a clip, apparently from Baron Coleman, showing surviving Discord messages from February 2026 about payment for an "Erika Kirk AmFest" campaign. In them users are told to delete messages, not to use Erika's name and to write "EK" instead. She ties a second Discord message about a "$10 incentive" for the "AL" (anti-left) clip of Erika crying after the White House Correspondents' Dinner shooting to the email she earlier reported, which directed that clip to be amplified. She says Blake Wynn, who she says was beside Erika that night, runs Enclave and Key, the firm she says supplied the Erika clip drive. Citing a tip and a 2004 article, she reports that Blake Wynn's father, Kenneth "Kenny" Wynn, brother of Steve Wynn, was the subject of a child pornography investigation under Operation Predator.

She says Val Peterson, UVU's vice president of facilities, once sat on the boards of Timpanogos Hospital and Provo airport. She asks for tips on the hospital's board and says Governor Cox's office has not answered her question about a September 10 meeting with Peterson. After the ads she covers Zacharia's exit and her October 16 tweet praising Trump, and plays a Tim Dillon Show segment satirizing Zacharia's exit and Pete Hegseth's proposed livestreamed execution of the Fort Hood shooter. She says parents have emailed her about Erika Kirk being billed as a special guest at a pre-K through 12 Christian school's Veterans Day chapel, and she shows a redacted flyer. In the comments segment she answers questions on her maternity leave and a viewer's note on TPUSA's $10,000 statue reward, and closes with merch plugs and a callback to the "gang bangers" bit.
