## (a) Timestamps
`check_claim_timestamps.py` now estimates when a snippet is spoken: exact at a transcript marker, interpolated by character position inside a block, and with the episode's duration closing the last block. A claim may start at most 20s after and 75s before the words. **500 claim timestamps** were set to 5s before the estimated words (never before the block start). Of the baseline's 24 timestamp findings, 11 were caught this way; the rest are corrected by hand below.

Only ep11 remains off (11 claims, in `config/claim_timestamp_baseline.json`): its transcript has gaps and no markers after 00:49:16, so it waits for the Phase 2 re-fetch.
