# Attribution pass (ROADMAP 2.5c), 2026-10-15

The QA baseline found claims that state the host's allegations, or her reading of documents and footage, as fact (8 of 278 sampled). The archive records what is alleged or presented in the episodes, so every claim must say who asserts it.

**Selection.** All 652 claims whose Claim sentence named no speaker and had no attribution verb.

**Rewrite.** Sonnet reviewers, each given the claim and the transcript around it, attributed each claim to the host, a clip, a guest, a viewer comment, a document or footage. Counts: host 319, document 132, clip 128, footage 39, comment 19, guest 14, other 1. Titles that stated an allegation as fact were rewritten too (531).

**Checks.**
- A rewrite was applied automatically only if it kept every number, capitalised name and quoted phrase of the original (617).
- The other 35 were read by hand and applied. Most only replaced wording like "Episode presents"; seven also corrected content toward the transcript: C-1162, C-1190, C-1195, C-1218, C-1669, C-3467, C-3469. For example, C-1195's "listed on Amazon before September 10" is a viewer comment the host read.
- One reviewer addition was removed (C-2335, "which she calls impossible").

After the pass, no claim lacks a speaker. Every rewrite is in `rewrites.csv`, with the reviewer's note. Notes that flag a possible transcription error (e.g. C-2291, "Gorg Fastbinder" / "Helmet Becker") feed the Phase 2 name sweep.
